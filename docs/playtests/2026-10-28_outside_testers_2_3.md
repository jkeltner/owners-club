## Observations

- Both testers were adults. The experienced tester asked the most basic question ("what is the market?"), so the fold-out is for everyone, not only new players.
- The experienced tester's thesis ("Nvidia will continue to benefit from the huge computing capacity boom") again skipped the P/E and priced-in points the coach raised. It's the second tester to do this; keep watching the thesis recap.
- The first ✎ note about the social side was positive; nobody has yet complained about being compared with friends.

## Decisions

- Profit is phrased as "keeps X¢ of each $1 as profit", never "earns".
- The market explainer is folded by default for every experience level.
- The logo stays a placeholder until a design pass.
- Name spelling (decided by the project lead): "Owners Club", no apostrophe, a descriptive plural like Diners Club.
